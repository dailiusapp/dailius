# Product Principles

## Principle 1

Dailius is not a calendar replacement.

It sits above calendars.

---

## Principle 2

The AI should optimize life, not just schedule empty slots.

A good plan considers:

- Goals
- Energy
- Recovery
- Preferences
- Commitments
- Constraints

---

## Principle 3

The user remains in control.

The AI should:

- Explain recommendations
- Suggest changes
- Allow adjustments

Avoid making unexpected changes.

---

## Principle 4

Simple beats sophisticated.

Prefer:

- Fast shipping
- Simple architecture
- Real user feedback

Over:

- Complex AI systems
- Perfect optimization
- Premature scalability
