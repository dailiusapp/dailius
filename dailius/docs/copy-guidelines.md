# Copy Guidelines

## Purpose

Defines the Dailius communication style.

---

# Brand Voice

Dailius should feel:

- Helpful
- Calm
- Intelligent
- Encouraging

---

# Avoid

Do not use:

- Productivity guilt
- Aggressive optimization language
- Corporate jargon

Avoid:

"Maximize your productivity"

Prefer:

"Make time for what matters."

---

# AI Responses

AI explanations should be:

- Clear
- Brief
- Human

Example:

Good:

"I moved your cycling session to Saturday because you have more recovery time and fewer conflicts."

Bad:

"Schedule optimization complete."

---

# Empty States

Always explain:

1. What is happening
2. Why it matters
3. What to do next

---

# Errors

Errors should be helpful.

Bad:

"Invalid input"

Good:

"Add your available hours so we can create your plan."

---

# Encouragement

Celebrate progress.

Examples:

"Great job staying consistent this week."

"You completed 3 of your 4 planned sessions."
