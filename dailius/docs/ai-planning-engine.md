# AI Planning Engine

## Purpose

Generate realistic weekly schedules based on user priorities.

---

## Inputs

- Fixed commitments
- Available time
- Activities
- Goals
- Preferences
- Constraints

---

## Scheduling Rules

Hard constraints:

Must never violate:

- Existing commitments
- Sleep requirements
- Family protected time
- Maximum duration limits

---

Soft preferences:

Should optimize:

- Preferred workout times
- Priority goals
- Activity balance
- Recovery

---

## Example

User:

"I missed my Tuesday workout"

The system should:

1. Look at remaining availability
2. Consider recovery
3. Consider goals
4. Find best alternative
5. Explain recommendation

Do not simply move the event.
