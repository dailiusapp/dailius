# MVP Scope

## Goal

A stranger can sign up, create their commitments and goals, receive a useful weekly plan, and ask AI to adjust it when life changes.

---

# Included Features

## Authentication

- User signup/login
- User profile

## Onboarding

Users define:

### Fixed commitments

Examples:

- Work
- Childcare
- Appointments
- School
- Family events

### Flexible activities

Examples:

- Running
- Cycling
- Strength training
- Guitar
- Reading
- Errands

### Goals

Examples:

- Run faster 5K
- Exercise 4 times/week
- Practice guitar 3 times/week

### Constraints

Examples:

- Maximum 40 minutes weekdays
- Longer sessions weekends
- No exercise after 8 PM
- Protect family dinner
- Maintain sleep

---

## Planning

System generates:

- Weekly schedule
- Calendar-style view
- Explanation of decisions

---

## AI Interaction

Users can say:

"Move my workout"

"I missed Tuesday's workout"

"Prioritize running for the next 12 weeks"

"I have to work late Wednesday"

AI proposes a new plan.

---

# Not Included in MVP

Do NOT build:

- Mobile apps
- Wearable integrations
- Advanced ML models
- Household accounts
- Social features
- Autonomous AI agents
- Complex optimization algorithms
