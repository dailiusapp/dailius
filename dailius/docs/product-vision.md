# Dailius Product Vision

## Mission

Dailius is an AI Life Planner that helps people make better use of their limited time.

It is not another calendar.

Calendars answer:
"What do I have scheduled?"

Task managers answer:
"What do I need to do?"

Dailius answers:

"Given everything happening in my life, what should I do and when is the best time to do it?"

---

## Vision

Build an AI-powered personal planning layer that understands:

- Calendar commitments
- Personal goals
- Recurring activities
- Constraints
- Preferences
- Changing circumstances

The AI continuously helps users reconcile what they want to accomplish with the reality of their available time.

---

## Initial Product

AI Personal Time Planner.

The first version helps users:

1. Connect or enter their commitments
2. Define goals
3. Define flexible activities
4. Define availability and constraints
5. Generate an optimized weekly plan
6. Adjust when life changes

---

## Founder Use Case

The initial test user is a busy professional balancing:

- Full-time technology career
- Family responsibilities
- Cycling
- Running
- Strength training
- Guitar practice
- Reading
- Personal projects

The product should solve the problem:

"I want to accomplish many meaningful things, but I need help figuring out how everything fits together."
